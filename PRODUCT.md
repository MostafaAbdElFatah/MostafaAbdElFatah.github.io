# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential clients evaluating Mostafa for senior iOS and mobile engineering work.

## Product Purpose

Present Mostafa's engineering experience and project work so visitors can assess fit and contact him or download his CV.

## Positioning

The portfolio shows both commercial mobile products and officially authorized platforms, including Quran, hadith, and education software, alongside the engineering work behind them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Visitors can browse featured projects and the broader project catalogue, follow app-store links, reach social and contact destinations, and download the CV.
- The site supports dark and light themes.
- Preserve factual project descriptions, career metrics, Arabic content, and all existing destinations and interactions. Do not invent claims.

## Brand Commitments

- The supplied visual references are the direction for the site redesign: retain the MA identity and provide dark and light presentations.
- Existing MA marks, portrait, project imagery, and icon assets are in `src/assets/images/` and `public/icons.svg`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project descriptions, career metrics, Arabic text, and links are present in `src/pages/index.html`.
- The existing CV is `public/docs/Mostafa-Abdel-Fattah-CV.pdf`.
- Brand and project artwork is documented in `src/assets/README.md`.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, client endorsements, or performance claims.

## Product Principles

- Make relevant engineering experience easy to evaluate.
- Keep claims and project details grounded in the existing portfolio.
- Keep contact and CV actions direct.
- Preserve access to the full project catalogue and its original destinations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Preserve semantic page structure, keyboard-accessible links, and the language and right-to-left direction markers on Arabic content.
